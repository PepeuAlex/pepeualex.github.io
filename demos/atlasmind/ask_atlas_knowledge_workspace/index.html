What is our updated remote work policy for 2024?
A
AtlasMind

Based on the recent updates to the employee handbook, the 2024 remote work policy has transitioned to a structured hybrid model for most departments.

Employees are now expected to be in the office for a minimum of three days per week [1]. Tuesday and Thursday are designated as mandatory "anchor days" for company-wide collaboration, while the third day can be chosen in agreement with team managers.

Fully remote arrangements are still available but require explicit VP-level approval and are generally reserved for specific technical roles or extenuating circumstances [2]. Additionally, a new "Work from Anywhere" benefit allows employees to work remotely from any location for up to four weeks per calendar year.
